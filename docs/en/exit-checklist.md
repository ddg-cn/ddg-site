# Responsible Exit Checklist

Pick who you are and, if you like, which situation you are in. The list shows the steps that apply to you, in order. It is drawn from the [Responsible Exit Guide](./exit-guide), the ACT Responsible Exit Policy and Checklist (2022), the FLA retrenchment guidance (2023), ETI's Technical Guidance on Responsible Disengagement (2026) and Chinese law. The reference after each step tells you where it comes from.

::: tip Situations
**S1** a buyer ends or sharply cuts orders · **S2** a factory ends a customer · **S3** a factory closes · **S4** a factory relocates · **S5** an exit forced by sanctions, an import ban or a conflicting law. See the [guide](./exit-guide) for each.
:::

*General information, not legal advice. Check current law before acting.*
