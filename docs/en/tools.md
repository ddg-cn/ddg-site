# 🧰 Tools

Every checker runs in your browser: the file is read on your computer and is not uploaded. Each one lists the findings on the page and exports them to Excel.

## Report quality checkers

| Tool | Checks | Input | 中文 |
|------|--------|-------|------|
| [🧾 **Report QC**](/en/report-qc.html) | Drop any mix of Clarity, RBA, SMETA, amfori BSCI, EcoVadis and CDP PDFs; the type of each file is detected and checked. Each report type has its own page with the rules and what the figures mean (such as the RBA estimated score), linked from Report QC | PDF (several, mixed) | [中文](/zh/tools/report-qc.html) |
| [📚 Policy Consistency Check](/en/policy-qc.html) | A set of policies, manuals, procedures and forms: the same rule with different numbers, wrong document names or codes in references, terms defined two ways, retention periods, PRC statutory minimums | .docx / PDF / .txt (several) | [中文](/zh/tools/policy-qc.html) |
| [🌡️ Carbon Footprint Form Check](/en/carbon-qc.html) | Product carbon footprint forms (EU battery CFR template, customer PCF templates, LCI data sheets), checked against a standard you pick (EU battery, PEF, ISO 14067, GHG Protocol, PACT, Catena-X, TfS): dataset vs component, suggested TeR / GeR / TiR / P with reasons, DQR arithmetic, factors vs public ranges, quantity × EF | .xlsx / .xlsm (several) | [中文](/zh/tools/carbon-qc.html) |
| [🧪 SLCP Verifier Tool QC](/en/slcp-qc.html) | Filled offline SLCP Verifier Tool 1.7.x, including the SLCP platform's automated data quality checks | .xlsm | [中文](/zh/tools/slcp-qc.html) |

## Analysis and reference

| Tool | What it does | 中文 |
|------|--------------|------|
| [🗂️ Report Data Extractor](/en/data-extract.html) | Turn many Clarity, RBA, SMETA, BSCI, EcoVadis, CDP reports, SLCP tools and carbon footprint forms into one Excel table per type, with the columns you pick; nothing is uploaded or stored | [中文](/zh/tools/data-extract.html) |
| [📊 ESG Report Lens](/en/esg-lens.html) | Spot-check a sustainability / ESG report: numbers that don't add up, claims that need evidence, gaps against reporting standards; A–E content rating | [中文](/zh/tools/esg-lens.html) |
| [🥬 Farm Produce Carbon Calculator](/en/farm-carbon.html) | Quick farm-gate footprint for vegetables, fruit, tea and grain from fertiliser, fuel, power and film use; IPCC 2019 Tier 1 field N2O and paddy CH4; editable factors, Excel export | [中文](/zh/tools/farm-carbon.html) |
| [🗺️ Climate & ESG Ratings at a Glance](/en/esg-glance.html) | SBTi targets by region, sector and size; CDP A List; EcoVadis medals; IRMA mines; RMI smelters. Filters for your own angle; refreshed yearly | [中文](/zh/tools/esg-glance.html) |
| [📈 Social Compliance Programs at a Glance](/en/peer-programs.html) | Audit volumes and reach of SLCP, SMETA, amfori BSCI, RBA, SA8000 and other programs | [中文](/zh/tools/peer-programs.html) |
