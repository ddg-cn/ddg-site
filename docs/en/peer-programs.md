# Social Compliance Programs at a Glance

How many audits or assessments each major social compliance program reports per year, and how far it reaches: members, countries, facilities and workers covered. Every figure comes from the program's own annual report, public filing or website. The figures are updated each quarter when new reports come out.

## Reading the numbers

- **Each program counts its own unit.** SLCP counts verified assessments, RBA counts Validated Assessment Program (VAP) audits, and SA8000 counts certified facilities, FLA counts its own assessments of a sample of members' factories, and Higg FEM counts verified environmental assessments (it is the environmental counterpart to SLCP, not a social audit). The others count audits.
- **"Members" is not comparable across programs.** It means signatories for SLCP, member companies for amfori and RBA, all Sedex members (buyers and suppliers) for SMETA, brand and retailer partners for Better Work, participating companies for FLA, and member brands for Fair Wear.
- **Better Work and Fair Wear publish no global yearly totals.** Their figures are the counters on their own websites at the date shown (earlier years from archived copies of the site), so they are snapshots rather than annual totals. Fair Wear's factory count is the factories its member brands disclose.
- **Only reported figures are shown.** Years a program did not report are left as gaps; nothing is estimated.
- Hover a point on the chart for its value. The source column links to the latest report used.
