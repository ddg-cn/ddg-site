# 🗺️ Climate & ESG Ratings at a Glance

Two groups of schemes side by side. **Climate targets, commitments & ESG ratings**: who sets science-based targets, who makes the CDP A List, how EcoVadis medals work, and which companies have committed to 100% renewable electricity through RE100. **Responsible minerals due diligence**: which mines IRMA has assessed and which smelters and refiners RMI lists; both cover social and environmental issues at site level. Use the filters and **Break down by** buttons to look at the SBTi and RMI data from your own angle, for example net-zero uptake by sector in Asia, or 1.5°C ambition among SMEs. This page is refreshed once a year.

## Reading the numbers

- **Each scheme measures something different.** SBTi validates targets; CDP scores disclosure; EcoVadis rates management systems on a curve; RE100 is a commitment, and its annual report checks progress against RE100's own criteria; IRMA audits mine sites; RMI lists smelters and refiners that passed a due diligence assessment. A company can appear in all of them, or none.
- **SBTi, RE100 and RMI figures** are counted from the full public lists (one company or facility per row) on the date shown; the RE100 electricity figures come from its annual disclosure report. **CDP, EcoVadis and IRMA figures** are the totals the schemes published; the IRMA locations are approximate.
- **Shares** (net-zero, 1.5°C, removed) are shown only for groups of at least 20 companies, so a few companies cannot swing the picture.
