## Reading the numbers

- **Each scheme measures something different.** SBTi validates targets; CDP scores disclosure; EcoVadis rates management systems on a curve; IRMA audits mine sites; RMI lists smelters and refiners that passed a due diligence assessment. A company can appear in all of them, or none.
- **SBTi and RMI figures** are counted from the full public lists (one company or facility per row) on the date shown. **CDP, EcoVadis and IRMA figures** are the totals the schemes published; the IRMA locations are approximate.
- **Shares** (net-zero, 1.5°C, removed) are shown only for groups of at least 20 companies, so a few companies cannot swing the picture.
